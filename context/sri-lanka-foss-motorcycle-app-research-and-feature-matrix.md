# Sri Lanka FOSS Motorcycle App — Research & Feature Matrix

*Source: live web search on Sept 13, 2026. No training data / memory used.*
*Context: growing Sri Lanka motorcycle community, new riders, new brands, expanding industry. Goal: free, open-source (FOSS) app for Sri Lanka combining all features of top existing apps and beyond.*

## 1. TL;DR

- **No free, open-source, all-in-one motorcycle app specifically for Sri Lanka exists today** (per live search).
- Closest local things are transport hailing, rentals, tourist safety, transit, marketplaces, tour operators — not rider navigation + safety + community.
- Global FOSS moto projects exist but are tiny/early and not LK-specific: PeakMoto (AGPL-3, planned), openGpx (MIT, self-hosted), SteedPilot/MotoNav/BikeNavESP32 (BLE HUD DIY).
- Opportunity is real: combine global best features + LK localization (Sinhala/Tamil/English, offline hill-country, monsoon/Poya, LK hazards, fuel/EV, service network for Bajaj/Hero/Honda/Yamaha/KTM/Voge/Zonsen/Lifan).

### REVER — rever.co/pro

- Free: discover 1000s routes, track rides & stats, connect riders, custom communities, challenges, friends on map, route-line nav, rider news, Dyrt campsites, Knuckle HQ events, GoPro iOS, GPX import.
- PRO: Butler Maps (Epic/Best/Great/ADV/Paved Trails/Lost Hwys, hand-ridden), Twisty Routing, ADV Planner (100 wpts, no voice, paved+dirt), GPX export, 3D maps/flyover, offline maps/routes, weather radar/alerts, PDF maps, local POIs, TBT (A/B, 25 wpts, avoid toll/ferry/hwy), LiveRIDE + Safety SMS + live link, privacy zones, auto-pause, hide max speed. 3000+ maps. Works worldwide, airplane-mode record.
- Scale: 1M+ downloads, 4.5/16.9K Play Jun 22, 2026, 4.7/16K Apple.

### calimoto — calimoto.com/pricing

- Standard vs Premium. Premium: twisty algo (fast/winding/no-hwy/twisty + forest/mountain/desert/coast env), round-trip generator free-to-plan, desktop+app planning, offline maps, TBT voice, GPX/KML/ITN import+export, ridden blue lines map, synced group + live location, ride record, speed + speed-limit display, lean/accel stats, fixed-camera alerts. No live traffic. US DB weaker per reviews. 4.6/5 16K+ claim.

### Scenic — scenic.app / premium / Play Aug 13, 2026

- Via-points/stops, curvy + AI beauty modes, round-trip by distance/direction, 1000s public rated routes, import GPX/KML/KMZ/GDB/ITN/Google URL/Furkot, export GPX, route/location folders, TBT + voice + detour behavior, glove-friendly + handlebar controllers, CarPlay/Android Auto (incl Carpuride W702), day/night/satellite/hybrid styles, traffic/safety-camera/3D layers, POIs fuel/EV/moto/food/hotel, dirt viz, elevation, SCDB cameras, closures/incidents, global offline, track speed/loc/elev + photos + stats + social web page. Claim #1 rated, 30M hrs, 200k+ routes.

### Kurviger

- Best web planner free no account, 4 modes fastest-extra curvy, round trips 300km free / 600km sub, GPX/ITN/KML mature, Tourer/Tourer+ pricing above, one sub web+app, CarPlay/Android Auto recent, offline but reroute/search needs online, no cameras (founder decision since 2019), no live traffic, closures via map data (late). Very technical/customizable.

### Motobit — getmotobit Jul 27, 2026

- 100k+ riders, 4.6/3500+ Play, 4.7 App Store. 4 routing methods, rural-road preference, audio/display nav + auto-recalc, personalized hazard warnings (bends/damaged/speed checks) free, unlimited tracking, point-by-point cornering-style + lean/accel graphs map-linked, predictive corner assistant, CarPlay/Android Auto, frequent updates, free plan+nav to 100km.

### Cardo Ride (ex-Riser, acquired 2023)

- Relaunch Apr 21, 2026. 200M km / 130M miles tracked, 1000s groups. Track, discover, plan, share, personalized newsfeed, reWind 3D recap, Group for all Cardo riders + supercurvy, Cardo Connect device link, deeper analytics roadmap. Free + Pro trial. PACKTALK PRO HW crash detection (helmet sensors + phone + cloud) separate.

### Detecht — detechtapp.com

- Sweden 2018, Chalmers algo, 25k+ subs, €400k/$452k raise May 2026 Chalmers Ventures. Auto crash detect via phone sensors → contacts + position, safety tracking live share, hazard warnings. Trip Planner curvy/round-trip, stops/waypoints, 100k+ user routes, moto TBT voice. Community/roadbook/profile, stats, GPX io. Premium unlocks TBT, Round Trips, Curvy, Safety Tracking, 5 contacts, GPX.

### EatSleepRIDE — eatsleepride.com

- Free + CRASHLIGHT $49/yr. AI crash detect + slide-cancel, contacts via SMS/call/email worldwide (needs GPS+cell+data). Explore nearby rider-shared roads, privacy trim, moto POIs, offline record/replay scrub (dist/speed/lean/elev), live web-code tracking, private Group Ride live map, private-by-default + GDPR, challenges/check-ins/points/leaderboards/prizes, partners Amazon/Voom/Riders-share/Bosch/CSC.

### Beeline — beeline.co

- Companion for Moto II/Velo 2 + standalone. 8M+ rides, 223M miles. Quiet/fun/fast + community ratings, multi-stop, mid-ride edit, GPX/komoot/Strava import, share + ride together, heatmap/history, Strava/Health sync, offline, compass arrow + Route TBT + Tracking modes, spoken audio, device buttons skip/pause/rate, Plus: live traffic, loop from distance, Shuffle, 3D, cameras, 1-mo free. 4.6-4.7 ratings.

## 5. Beyond — what LK FOSS should add

1. Trilingual offline-first: Sinhala/Tamil/English UI+voice; OSM LK extract + hill-country packs; low-data + battery-saver for cheap Androids.
2. LK road reality: monsoon/weather + Poya/holiday closures + works; hazards potholes/buses/dogs/elephants/checkpoints; LK limits/cameras; fuel prices + EV charge for Revolt.
3. New-rider/new-brand layer: learner theory, service/spares/dealer dir for Bajaj/Hero/Honda/Yamaha/KTM/Voge/Zonsen/Lifan/Haojue, per-brand owner groups, Riyasewana-like marketplace link, insurance/reg help.
4. SOS that works on LK networks: phone-sensor detection with SMS fallback, 119/1990 + family, live trip link, privacy zones.
5. LK community routes: Colombo-Galle, Kandy-Nuwara Eliya, Ella, Ancient Cities loops, GPX interoperable with REVER/Scenic/Kurviger.

Start: fork PeakMoto AGPL-3 for mobile + openGpx MIT GraphHopper/BRouter curvy logic, seed OSM LK + community routes. Keeps forever-free vs $39-59/yr incumbents.
